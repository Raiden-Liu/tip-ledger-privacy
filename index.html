tip-ledger Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

tip-ledger is an app for logging tips and shift income. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter. The short version: it stays on your phone.

Your data is stored only on this phone

Everything you enter — jobs, shifts, amounts, hours, notes and settings — is saved in a database on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

We have no servers and no accounts

tip-ledger has no sign-up, no login and no server of its own. Because there is nowhere for your data to go, we cannot see it, and neither can anyone else.

No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads

The app contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting software, and it does not use any third-party service that collects data. It needs no internet connection for any free feature.

The only thing that leaves your phone is a purchase

If you buy tip-ledger Pro, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app only learns from the store whether Pro has been purchased, and keeps that status on your device. The stores' own privacy policies apply to the purchase.

Things that happen only when you ask

  • Exports. When you export a spreadsheet or a backup file, the file is created on your device and handed to the share sheet you choose. Where it goes next is up to you.
  • Reminders. Daily reminders are scheduled on your device by the operating system. Nothing is sent over the network.
  • Rating and feedback. “Rate tip-ledger” opens the store's own rating prompt or page. “Send feedback” opens your email app with a new message; we only receive what you choose to send.

Phone backups

Your phone's own backup service (Google backup on Android, iCloud or computer backup on iPhone) may include the app's data, under your account and your phone's settings. tip-ledger does not run or control those backups.

Children

tip-ledger is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes its data from your phone. Copies you exported, and your phone's own backups, are kept or deleted under your control.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
