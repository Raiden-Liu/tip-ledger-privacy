Tip Ledger — Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

Tip Ledger ("the app") is an offline tip and shift income tracker published by Raiden Liu ("we"). This policy covers the Android and iOS apps.

The short version: we do not collect, receive, or share any of your data.

1. Your data lives on your phone

Everything you enter — jobs, shifts, tips, wages, hours, notes, and settings — is saved in a database on your device. The app never sends this data anywhere; the Android app does not even request permission to use the network. Your phone's own backup may keep a copy under your account — see "Device backups" below.

2. No servers, no accounts

We run no servers and there is no account to create. We cannot see your data, and neither can anyone else through us. For the same reason we cannot restore it for you: if you delete the app or lose your phone, the data stored in the app is gone unless you kept your own copy.

3. No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads

The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or other third-party tracking software. It does not use advertising identifiers and does not track you across apps or websites.

4. Only the app store is involved, and only about the app

If you buy Tip Ledger Pro, the payment is handled entirely by the app store you use (Google Play or the Apple App Store) under that store's own privacy policy. We never receive your name, email address, or payment details; the app only learns from the store whether the purchase exists.

On Android, Google Play also checks that the app was installed from Google Play (see "Google Play licence check" under Permissions). That check is between your phone and Google Play and contains nothing you entered in the app.

What you choose to send yourself

  • Exports. When you export a file (for example a CSV of your shifts), the app hands it to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes after that — email, cloud storage, another app — is your choice and is covered by that service's privacy policy.
  • Feedback. "Send feedback" opens your own mail app with our address filled in. We see only what you decide to send, and use it only to reply and to improve the app.
  • Store ratings. Rating the app happens in the app store, under the store's policy.

Device backups

Your phone's operating system may include app data in the device backups you have turned on (for example Google's backup on Android or iCloud Backup on iOS). Those backups are made by your phone, go to your own Google or Apple account, and are controlled in your phone's settings. We have no access to them.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): used only to show the shift reminder you set up. Reminders are scheduled on the device itself; no push service is involved.
  • Run at startup (Android): lets the phone re-schedule your reminder after a restart.
  • Vibration (Android): lets a reminder vibrate when it arrives, like any other notification.
  • Google Play billing (Android): lets you buy Tip Ledger Pro through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; the app only learns whether the purchase exists (see section 4).
  • Google Play licence check (Android): added by Google Play to the apps it distributes. It asks Google Play whether this copy of the app was installed from Google Play. It does not read or send anything you entered in the app.

The app asks for no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Children

The app is intended for working adults and is not directed at children. Because we collect no data from anyone, we hold no personal information about children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date. A change that affects how your data is handled will also be noted in the app's release notes.
